# QR Menu Guide for Restaurants and Cafés
A short, practical guide to QR code menus and table ordering for dine-in restaurants and cafés.

# What is a QR menu?
Each table has a QR code. A guest scans it with their phone camera, the menu opens in the browser, and they can browse and order. No app to download https://tastezaika.com/ .

# Why owners use it
Menu and price changes go live instantly, with no reprinting
Guests place their own orders, so fewer mix-ups
Orders reach the kitchen dashboard in real time
Veg and non-veg marks and allergen filters help guests choose
Reports show peak hours and top dishes

# Quick start checklist
Add your dishes, prices and photos
Add your tables and print a QR stand for each
Place a test order from your own phone
Roll out to one section first, then the whole floor
Keep a few paper menus as a backup
# Good to know
QR ordering suits dine-in places. It supports your team instead of replacing it, and it is not a delivery tool https://tastezaika.com/.
